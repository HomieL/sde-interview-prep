# Daily plan

1. Read `progress/log.md`, `progress/weak-spots.md`, and the "Upcoming interviews"
   section of `progress/log.md`. Note how many days remain until the next interview.
2. Build a plan that fits the time I have today (ask if I didn't say):
   - 1 retry: a problem I failed or needed hints on 3–7 days ago
   - 1–2 new problems weighted toward weak spots, otherwise toward core Google
     topics: arrays/hashing, two pointers, sliding window, binary search, trees,
     graphs (BFS/DFS, topological sort), heaps, intervals, DP
   - 1 cheat sheet to skim, matching today's weakest area
3. In the final 3 days before an interview: no new hard topics. Do 1–2 full mocks,
   retries of past misses, and a behavioral run-through.
4. Output a short checklist I can follow. Name well-known problems so I can find
   them on LeetCode.
